# Data Rights and Publication Boundary

The MIT License applies to original source code only. It does not license market
data, index data, provider responses, trademarks, or third-party documentation.

The repository includes deterministic synthetic fixtures and selected aggregate
research outputs. Raw, static, manually curated, and processed A-share market
data are excluded. The formal OOS figures were produced from private licensed
inputs and cannot be regenerated from the public fixture.

See `data/DATA_MANIFEST.md` for file-level classifications. The synthetic data
must not be described as real market history, and the aggregate OOS outputs must
not be described as live performance, capacity evidence, or an investment offer.
